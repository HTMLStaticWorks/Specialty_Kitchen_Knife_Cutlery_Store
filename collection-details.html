SERIES PHILOSOPHY

Japanese Craft Series

The Geometry of Pure Slicing. Designed around the classical Sakai tradition of forward kinetic balance, ultra-thin spine tapers, and surgical push-cutting precision.

STEEL ALLOY SG2 Micro-Powder / VG10
HARDNESS 61.5 – 62.5 HRC
BEVEL ANGLE 15° Symmetrical
HANDLE STYLE Octagonal Wa-Handle

SG2 Micro-Powder Steel

Unlike conventionally cast steels that contain irregular carbide clusters, SG2 is atomized into microscopic powder grains before hot isostatic pressing. This dense, microscopic crystalline matrix enables an edge that remains keen twice as long as traditional stainless alloys.

Octagonal Wa Ergonomics

Carved from natural Teak and stabilized Ebony with subtle eight-faceted geometry. The facets align naturally with the proximal joints of your fingers, providing positive indexing without requiring excessive hand grip pressure.

Intended Culinary Focus

Tailored for delicate ingredient preparation: slicing tuna, carpaccio, trimming shallots, julienning carrots, and chiffonading basil. The razor-fine apex glides through cell walls without rupturing moisture, locking natural juices inside the food.
