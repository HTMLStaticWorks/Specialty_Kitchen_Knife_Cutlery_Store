Care & Maintenance Protocol

Recommended Ownership Rituals

Hand Wash Exclusively

Never subject to the dishwasher. Mild dish soap, soft non-scratch sponge, warm water.

Immediate Towel Dry

Wipe clean and dry immediately with a clean cotton towel before returning to magnetic storage or sheath.

Weekly Honing Alignment

Maintain apex straightness using a 2,000-grit ceramic honing rod at a consistent 15° stroke angle.

Wooden Board Pairing

Pair with end-grain walnut or soft Japanese rubber cutting boards. Avoid glass and stone counters.
